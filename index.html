Junseob Kim

Ph.D. Student, Pierre R. Lamond Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Duke University

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Duke University, advised by Prof. Rishikesan Kamaleswaran in the Kamaleswaran Lab, and a Major in the Republic of Korea Army. My research develops AI for healthcare, with a focus on biomedical vision-language models, medical video understanding, and foundation models for electronic health records.
